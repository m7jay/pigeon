Subscribing and fan-out

Subscribe one of your endpoints to this type; the API validates each event's payload against the schema above before anything else happens, and the payload version is the one this page shows. Fan-out is the active endpoints of your consumer intersected with the active subscriptions for this type (FR-505, FR-507), so subscribing an endpoint is what makes it receive the type. Each delivery that fan-out produces is signed with its endpoint's own secret, retried independently, and kept with its full attempt trail - so what you see in the delivery log is what you receive.

Verifying what you receive

Signatures are Standard Webhooks. Take the endpoint's whsec_ secret to the snippets page for a verifier in curl, Go, Node or Python - during a rotation, accept the old and the new signature until the overlap window closes (UI-6).
